## Background-extraction prompts

Built-in image generation/editing was used, with transparent output enabled and the corresponding original reproduction as the edit target.

- Sunflowers (final pass): “Remove the beige paper and enclosed background gaps; preserve the original painted plant, stems, petals, leaves, colors, composition and brushwork. Use a clean transparent alpha background with no haze, glow, shadow or feathered backdrop. Remove the isolated signature. Do not add or redraw plants.”
- Field of Flowers: “Remove the cream/beige background and pale empty gaps, preserving the existing flowers, white petals, stems, leaves, brushwork, colors, and arrangement. Keep transparent negative space and an irregular plant silhouette. No new flowers, shadows, halos or rectangular backdrop.”

These are condensed records of the edit constraints. Original reproductions and the working sunflower alternatives are retained; the final sunflower and field-of-flowers cutouts are referenced by the website.

The original download URLs and reproduction-specific license information were not supplied. No museum-provided image download, reproduction license, or institutional endorsement is claimed. Visible captions link to the catalogue records; the underlying artworks and the supplied image files have distinct provenance.
